Tips for Best Results

  • 01
    Clear Product Image

    Place the product on a flat surface for a clear label photo

  • 02
    Good Lighting

    Ensure label is well-lit with no shadows or glare

  • 03
    Focus on Ingredient List

    Keep ingredients section fully visible and centered

  • 04
    Straight Angle

    Hold camera directly facing label — avoid tilted shots

  • 05
    Avoid Reflections

    Avoid flash on glossy packaging to prevent distortion

  • 06
    Full Label Visible

    Capture entire ingredient list — do not cut off text

  • 07
    Steady Hands

    Keep device steady to avoid blurry or unreadable text

  • 08
    Readable Text Size

    Zoom in on small ingredient lists for better OCR accuracy
